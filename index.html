Victor Atem Atem

Candidate for Governor of Warrap State

Victor Atem Atem is contesting for the governorship of Warrap State in South Sudan’s 2026 general election. His publicly stated priorities include peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance.

Public service

From Warrap State to national service

Victor Atem Atem has served in state and national government, including as Warrap State Minister for Local Government, Minister of Education, Governor of the former Gogrial State and Undersecretary in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs.

His 2026 governorship campaign returns his focus to Warrap State, where he has previously held senior public responsibilities.

Campaign priorities

Eight areas at the centre of the campaign

These priorities were announced when Victor Atem Atem declared his intention to contest for Governor of Warrap State in September 2026.

01

Peace & Reconciliation

Building stronger foundations for peaceful coexistence across Warrap State.

02

Security

Strengthening public safety and the conditions needed for stable communities.

03

Youth Empowerment

Expanding opportunities for young people to learn, work and contribute.

04

Education

Giving education a central place in Warrap State’s long-term development.

05

Infrastructure

Improving the physical connections and services communities depend on.

06

Healthcare

Supporting stronger and more accessible health services across the state.

07

Agriculture

Backing agriculture as a foundation for livelihoods and local economic growth.

Promoting representation, participation and accountable public leadership.
