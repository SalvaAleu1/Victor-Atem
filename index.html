Profile

Public service across county, state and national institutions

Public records trace Victor Atem Atem’s service through several levels of government. He served in the Warrap State cabinet, including as Minister for Local Government and later Minister of Education. In September 2017 he was appointed Governor of the then Gogrial State.

In January 2023, a presidential decree appointed him Undersecretary for Defence in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs. He left that position in April 2024 following another presidential decree. In September 2026, he declared his bid for the governorship of Warrap State.

This section is based on published records from Sudan Tribune, Radio Tamazuj, UNDP and Standard Zone News. Source links appear below.
