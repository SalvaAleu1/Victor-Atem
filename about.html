About Victor Atem Atem

Public service in Warrap State and at national level

Victor Atem Atem has held senior responsibilities in state and national government and is contesting for Governor of Warrap State in the 2026 general election.

Atem previously served in the Warrap State Government, including as Minister for Local Government and later Minister of Education.

In September 2017, he was appointed Governor of the former Gogrial State. During that period, the state administration worked with partners including UNDP and the Government of Japan on public-finance and revenue administration initiatives.

In January 2023, he was appointed Undersecretary for Defence in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs. He served until April 2024.

In September 2026, he publicly declared his intention to contest for Governor of Warrap State. His campaign has identified peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance as priority areas.
