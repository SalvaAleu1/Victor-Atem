Campaign & public life

Selected developments from Victor Atem Atem’s public service and 2026 Warrap State governorship campaign.

23 Sep 2026

Victor Atem Atem declares bid for Governor of Warrap State

Victor Atem Atem declared his intention to contest for Governor of Warrap State, placing peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance among the campaign’s priorities.

2026

Gogrial leaders conclude reconciliation process

Victor Atem Atem joined other senior Gogrial leaders in a reconciliation process aimed at resolving longstanding political differences and encouraging unity and peaceful coexistence.

Jan 2023

Appointed Undersecretary for Defence

Victor Atem Atem was appointed Undersecretary for Defence in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs.

Sep 2017

Appointed Governor of Gogrial State

Victor Atem Atem was appointed Governor of the former Gogrial State in September 2017.

2013

Appointed Warrap State Minister of Education

He moved from the Local Government portfolio to the Ministry of Education in a Warrap State cabinet reshuffle.

2008

Gogrial peace process

As Commissioner of Gogrial East County, Victor Atem Atem took part in the Kal Kuel peace process that brought community chiefs together during a period of serious intercommunal tension.
