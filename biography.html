Biography

Victor Atem Atem

South Sudanese public servant and political leader from Gogrial East County, Warrap State.

Early life and roots

Gogrial East

Victor Atem Atem comes from Gogrial East County in present-day Warrap State. His public life has remained closely tied to Gogrial and the wider Warrap region.

His public leadership record begins with local administration in Gogrial East, where he served as County Commissioner.

County leadership

Commissioner of Gogrial East

By 2008, Atem was serving as Commissioner of Gogrial East County during a period of serious communal tension in the area.

That year, he and Gogrial West Commissioner Wol Deng Aleu were among the leaders who brought community chiefs together during the Kal Kuel peace meeting in Tonj. The process helped halt a cycle of violence that had affected communities in Gogrial.

Warrap State Government

Local Government and Education

Atem later joined the Warrap State cabinet, serving as Minister for Local Government before moving to the Ministry of Education in a 2013 cabinet reshuffle.

He subsequently served in the government of the former Gogrial State, including as security adviser and later in the education and social welfare portfolio.

Governor of Gogrial State

2017–2020

Victor Atem Atem was appointed Governor of the former Gogrial State on 18 September 2017.

His tenure included work on security, reconciliation, local administration, education and state revenue. In 2019, he joined the governors of Wau and Tonj in a tri-state peace and reconciliation process addressing intercommunal violence across the region.

He also supported the use of local radio for public education, peace messaging and community awareness.

Military and national service

Defence leadership

In 2019, Atem held the rank of Brigadier General. By 2026, he was publicly referred to as Major General Victor Atem Atem.

In January 2023, President Salva Kiir appointed him Undersecretary for Defence in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs. He served in that national role until April 2024.

2026

Warrap State governorship campaign

Victor Atem Atem is contesting for Governor of Warrap State under the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement (SPLM).

His campaign has identified peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance as its central priorities.

In 2026, he also participated with other senior Gogrial leaders in a reconciliation process aimed at resolving longstanding political differences and strengthening unity within the community.
