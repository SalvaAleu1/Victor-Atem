Biography

Victor Atem Atem

Victor Atem Atem is a South Sudanese public servant and political leader from Gogrial East County in Warrap State. His public career has included county administration, state government, governorship and national service in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs.

Early life and roots

Roots in Gogrial East

Victor Atem Atem comes from Gogrial East County in present-day Warrap State. His public life has remained closely connected to the communities of Gogrial and the wider Warrap region.

His earliest well-documented senior public role was as Commissioner of Gogrial East County. By 2008, he was serving in that position during a difficult period of communal tension in the area.

County leadership

Gogrial East and peace efforts

Local administration and reconciliation became important parts of his early public leadership.

As Commissioner of Gogrial East County, Atem took part in efforts to end fighting between communities in the Gogrial area. In 2008, he and the Commissioner of Gogrial West, Wol Deng Aleu, were among the leaders involved in bringing chiefs together during the Kal Kuel peace meeting in Tonj.

The agreement and subsequent peace efforts helped suspend a period of armed conflict that had affected communities in Gogrial.

State government

Ministerial service in Warrap State

Atem later served in senior state-government portfolios.

He served as Warrap State Minister for Local Government before moving to the Ministry of Education in a 2013 cabinet reshuffle. His work in state government placed him in portfolios dealing directly with local administration and education.

He later served in the government of the former Gogrial State, including as a security adviser and as Minister of Education and Social Welfare before his appointment as governor.

2017–2020

Governor of Gogrial State

On 18 September 2017, Victor Atem Atem was appointed Governor of the former Gogrial State.

His tenure included work on security and reconciliation, local administration, education, revenue administration and cooperation with development partners. In 2019, he joined the governors of Wau and Tonj in a tri-state peace and reconciliation process aimed at addressing intercommunal violence and strengthening peaceful coexistence.

During the same period, he publicly supported the use of local radio and community communication to promote peace, education and public awareness.

National service

Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs

In January 2023, President Salva Kiir appointed Victor Atem Atem Undersecretary for Defence in the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs.

He served in the national ministry until April 2024.

2026

Warrap State governorship campaign

Victor Atem Atem is contesting for Governor of Warrap State under the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement (SPLM).

His campaign has placed peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance among its central priorities.

In 2026, he also took part with other senior Gogrial leaders in a reconciliation process aimed at resolving longstanding political differences and supporting greater unity within the community.
