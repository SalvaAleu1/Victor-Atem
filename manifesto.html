Campaign agenda

Priorities for Warrap State

The campaign is centred on eight priorities: peace and reconciliation, security, youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture and inclusive governance.

01

Peace & Reconciliation

Promoting peaceful coexistence and reconciliation across communities in Warrap State.

02

Security

Supporting public safety and a stable environment for families, communities and economic activity.

03

Youth Empowerment

Expanding opportunities for young people through skills, participation and pathways to productive work.

04

Education

Strengthening education as a foundation for long-term social and economic development.

05

Infrastructure

Improving the infrastructure that connects communities and supports public services and local commerce.

06

Healthcare

Giving attention to stronger and more accessible health services for communities across the state.

07

Agriculture

Supporting agriculture and rural livelihoods as major parts of Warrap State’s economy and food security.

08

Inclusive Governance

Promoting participation, representation and responsible public administration.

Warrap State

Leadership focused on peace, services and opportunity

The campaign agenda brings together security, community reconciliation, education, healthcare, infrastructure, agriculture, youth participation and inclusive public leadership as connected priorities for Warrap State.
